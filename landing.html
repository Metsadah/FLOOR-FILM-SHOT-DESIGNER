Everything pre-production, nothing else

Built by a filmmaker, for the week before the first shooting day.

Shot designer

Top-down floor plans with walls, doors, furniture, cast blocking, camera moves, dolly tracks and lens angles. Play the movement.

Lighting diagrams

Fresnels, HMIs, LED panels, tubes, domes and lanterns with real gel names, colour temperature and diffusion — and a gear list that writes itself.

Scripts & AV scripts

Paste a screenplay or an AV table, break it down into scenes with stills and regie notes, export to PDF or Word.

Storyboards & mood

Boards with stills, sub-boards, PDFs as pages, sticky notes — drag any still straight onto a camera as its frame.

Call sheets

Multi-day, multi-location call sheets with clickable phone numbers, addresses and e-mails, your logo, props and gear.

Works together

Invite your DoP or producer to co-edit, share read-only links with comment pins, or simply export a .floorproj file.
