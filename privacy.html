Floorboard — Privacy policy

Version 2026-09-25 · applies to the hosted (cloud) version of Floorboard

Who is responsible

Floorboard is operated by Gerrit Floor / In beeld met Floor (also trading as Zoutwater), Molenstraat 15, 8081 BB Elburg, the Netherlands (the “controller”). Questions or requests: info@inbeeldmetfloor.nl.

What we store, and why

  • Your email address and a password hash — required, to create and secure your account and let your projects follow you across devices. Legal basis: performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Optional profile details — name, address, phone number, profession. You choose whether to provide these; they are only used to prefill your own call sheets and crew cards. Legal basis: consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by clearing or deleting them.
  • Your project content — scenes, boards, schedules, call sheets, and any images or contact details you add to them. This is your working material; we store it so the app works. You are responsible for the personal data of others (cast, crew) that you put in your own projects.
  • Consent record — which version of this policy you accepted, and when.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The only things kept in your browser are your login session and the app’s own working data.

Where it is stored

All data is stored with our processor Supabase in the European Union (region: Ireland). Weather and sunrise lookups send only a place name or coordinates of your filming location to Open-Meteo — never personal data.

Sharing you control

  • Share links — if you create a read-only link, a snapshot of that project is readable by anyone who has the link, until you revoke it.
  • Co-editing — if you invite someone to a production, they can see and edit that production’s content, including its call sheets and contact lists.

How long we keep it

As short as the purpose allows (GDPR art. 5(1)(e)). Concretely:

Account, profile, productions, stills, rooms and scansUntil you delete them. Deleting a production removes its data; Delete my account removes everything of yours at once, immediately.

Sub-processors: Supabase (database, files, sign-in — Ireland), Netlify (serves the app), Resend (sign-in emails) and, for paying customers, the payment provider named at checkout. Each is bound by a data processing agreement. Your data is not sold and not used to train anything.

Other people’s data in your productions. Call sheets, cast and crew lists contain personal data of people who are not our users. You decide to store it and whom you share it with, so for that data you are the controller and we are your processor. Keep read-only links to productions with contact details short-lived, and revoke them when the job is done.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export, and erase your data — all of which you can do yourself in the app (Production ▾ → Account & privacy: edit profile, “Download my data”, “Delete my account”). You can also object to or restrict processing, and you have the right to complain to the Dutch supervisory authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority in your own EU country.

The local / self-hosted version

The standalone version of Floorboard stores everything in your own browser or on your own server. In that case we receive no data from you at all, and whoever hosts it is the controller.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, you will be asked to review it the next time you sign in. The version you accepted is recorded with your account.
