2 · The service

Floorboard is a browser application for film pre-production: floor plans, lighting diagrams, scripts, storyboards, call sheets and related documents. The hosted version stores your productions on our servers so you can sync between devices, share and co-edit.

  • You must be at least 16, or have permission from a parent or guardian.
  • One person per account. Team members get their own account and are invited into a production.
